# Privacy Policy

Takeru Group (Takeru Kogawa) ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in connection with the mobile application "One Million Steps Odyssey" ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA/CPRA).

## 1. Information We Collect, Purposes, and Legal Bases

### (1) Account Information
* **Data Collected**: Email address, display name (nickname), and unique user identifier (UID) provided via authentication providers (Apple, Google, etc.).
* **Purpose**: To authenticate your account, save your game progress to the cloud, and enable data restoration across devices.
* **Legal Basis (GDPR)**: Performance of a contract (necessary to provide the App's core services).

### (2) Health and Fitness Data
* **Data Collected**: Step count data accessed with your explicit permission via Apple HealthKit or Android Health Connect.
* **Purpose**: Solely to calculate your historical timeline progression, award step milestones, and display personal activity charts within the App.
* **Legal Basis (GDPR)**: Your explicit consent (granted via OS permissions and in-app authorization).
* **Strict Non-Disclosure**: Your health and step data is never shared with third parties, sold to data brokers, or used for advertising targeting.

### (3) Usage Diagnostics and Advertising Identifiers
* **Data Collected**: App version, language preference, crash logs, anonymized usage statistics, and mobile advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or AAID).
* **Purpose**: To diagnose bugs, maintain stability, improve user experience, and serve in-app advertisements.
* **Legal Basis (GDPR)**: Your explicit consent (via App Tracking Transparency / consent prompts) for personalized ads, and our legitimate interests for app security, analytics, and non-personalized ad delivery.

## 2. Third-Party Services and International Data Transfers

We utilize trusted third-party service providers to operate the App and serve advertisements. In doing so, your data may be transferred to and processed on servers located in Japan, the United States, or other jurisdictions where our providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place to protect your personal data:

* **Firebase (Google LLC)**: Used for authentication, cloud database storage (Cloud Firestore), file storage, and analytics.
* **Google AdMob (Google LLC)**: Used to display rewarded and interstitial ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device's privacy settings (e.g., iOS Tracking settings or Android Ad settings).

## 3. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location (including the EEA, the UK, and California, USA), you have specific rights regarding your personal data:

1. **Right to Access / Know**: You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.
2. **Right to Rectification**: You have the right to correct inaccurate personal data (you can edit your nickname directly in the App).
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: You can permanently delete your account and erase all associated server data at any time using the "Delete Account" button in the App's side menu.
4. **Right to Withdraw Consent & Restrict Processing**: You may revoke health data permissions or ad tracking consent at any time via your device settings.
5. **No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information (CCPA/CPRA)**: We do not "sell" your personal information to third parties. Furthermore, we do not use or disclose Sensitive Personal Information (such as health/step data) for any purpose other than providing the App's core features.
6. **Right to Non-Discrimination**: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.
7. **Right to Lodge a Complaint (EEA/UK)**: If you have concerns about our data practices, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete such information.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the App. When you delete your account, all associated records are permanently removed from our active servers.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal or operational changes. Any updates will be posted within the App or on our official website with an updated effective date.
